Tokens are the basic units of text processed by language models — approximately 1,000 tokens equal 750 words. Each request bills the full prompt (your question plus any retrieved documentation, conversation history, and attachments) plus the reply and any visible reasoning ("thinking") tokens. Reading your plan's usage summary shows exactly how those three add up.

When a rolling window fills up, CloudGPT tells you which window is exhausted and blocks new requests until it resets — you can upgrade for higher capacity at any time. Separately, if Google's own free-tier API quota is exhausted, you will see a "Gemini Free Tier Quota has Reached its Limit" notice; that is a temporary provider-side limit, usually cleared within a minute, and is not related to your plan.

All four windows are rolling: 5 hours, daily, weekly (7 days), and monthly (30 days), each measured from its own last reset. Your billing dashboard shows the current usage for every window.
