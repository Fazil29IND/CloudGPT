Privacy Policy & Data Protection

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly when registering and interacting with CloudGPT:

  • Account Information: Email address, hashed password, name, and OAuth profile identifiers.
  • Chat History & Prompts: Messages submitted during sessions to provide conversational context and AI responses.
  • Billing Details: Payment transactions processed securely via Stripe. We do not store raw credit card numbers.
  • Telemetry & Usage: Token counts, rate limit quotas, and application performance metrics.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to:

  • Provide, personalize, and improve the CloudGPT research workspace.
  • Track and enforce subscription token quotas accurately.
  • Authenticate sessions and protect against brute-force attacks and abuse.

3. Data Retention Policy

Conversation history and session messages are stored securely in PostgreSQL and Redis working memory. Messages are retained for active account review until you delete individual sessions or request account erasure.

4. GDPR & CCPA Compliance: Your Rights

Under GDPR (Articles 15, 17, 20) and CCPA, you have the right to access, export, or permanently erase your personal data at any time.

5. Third-Party AI Providers

When generating responses, anonymized prompt context is transmitted to upstream AI providers (e.g. Moonshot Kimi, Alibaba Qwen, Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude). None of your personal account information is shared with model providers for training.

For privacy inquiries or custom compliance requests, please email privacy@cloudgpt.local.
